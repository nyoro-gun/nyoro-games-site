Privacy Policy (Fold Dungeon)

Nyoro Games ("we", "us") sets out below how information about users is handled in the smartphone game "Fold Dungeon" (the "App").

1. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information such as your name, address, phone number or email address through the App. We also do not send or store any information about you on our own servers.

2. Information stored on your device

Your best floor, sound setting, language setting and the state of a run in progress are stored only on your own device. They are never sent to us. They are erased when you uninstall the App.

3. Advertising

The App shows ads using "Google AdMob", a service provided by Google LLC. These are rewarded video ads that you choose to watch yourself, in return for a revival, extra folds, a hint, or boss-floor preparation or bonuses. To deliver ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect and use your advertising ID, IP address, device information, how you use the App and similar data. Information collected this way is handled under Google's privacy policy.

When an AdMob ad cannot be shown, the App may show the same kind of rewarded ad through "Unity Ads", a service provided by Unity Technologies. To deliver ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud, Unity Ads may collect and use your advertising ID, IP address, device information, how you use the App and similar data. Information collected this way is handled under Unity's privacy policy.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Unity Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

In regions where it is required, the App shows a consent screen (Google's consent management platform), for example on first launch. No ads (neither AdMob nor Unity Ads) are requested until consent is given. In regions where consent must be revisitable, you can review your choice at any time with the "Privacy Policy" button in the Settings screen.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (on Android: Settings → Google → Ads).

4. Disclosure to third parties

We do not provide the information we obtain to third parties, except where required by law. Ad delivery as described above is the exception: AdMob (Google LLC) and Unity Ads (Unity Technologies) handle information within the scope described in section 3.

5. Children

The App is not primarily directed at children under 13.

6. Changes to this policy

We may review and change this policy when necessary. Changes take effect when they are posted on this page.
