Privacy Policy - A Night Connected by Footprints

Nyoro Games ("we", "us") explains below how information is handled in the smartphone game "A Night Connected by Footprints" (Japanese title: 足あとがつなぐ夜; the "App").

1. Information handled by us

The App has no account registration or fields for your name, address, phone number or email address. We do not send or store game progress on our own servers. Information handled by advertising services and information you voluntarily provide when contacting us are described below.

2. Information stored on your device

Normal stage completion and unlock status, daily stage completion and unlock status and dates of use, display language and sound volume are saved on your device. We do not receive this information. Sound is planned for a future update, but the volume preference is saved now. The App has no cloud progress synchronization or account linking.

You can erase this data by clearing the App's data in your device settings. Removal during uninstallation and OS backup or restoration depend on your device settings.

3. Advertising services

The App uses Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC. Rewarded ads are shown when you choose to watch an ad to request a puzzle hint or unlock a daily stage from a past date.

For ad delivery, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention, Google's advertising SDK may collect and share identifiers such as the advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, interactions such as app usage and ad views, and diagnostic information. An IP address may be used to estimate your general location. We do not independently collect these data on our own servers; Google's handling is governed by its policies. SDK initialization or other processing may handle information before an ad is displayed.

When production ads are used and regional or configuration requirements apply, Google's consent form is shown. If reviewing your choices is required, use "Privacy settings" on the daily screen when that option is displayed. Development versions using test ads do not show this consent form. You can manage your advertising ID and ad personalization in your device's advertising or privacy settings. The options and steps vary by device and OS.

4. Contact information and disclosure

If you contact us, we use the email address and message you send only as needed to respond and handle your inquiry. We do not voluntarily disclose this information to third parties unless required by law. See section 3 for Google's handling of advertising data. To ask about deleting information you provided in an inquiry, contact us below.

5. External links

Opening an external page such as this policy from the App launches your browser. The destination's hosting provider or other services may process IP addresses and access logs necessary to serve the page. Their own policies apply. This page is hosted on GitHub Pages (see the GitHub General Privacy Statement).

6. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy when the App or the services it uses change. The revised policy and its update date will be posted on this page.
