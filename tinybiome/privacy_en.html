Privacy Policy - Tiny Biome

Nyoro Games ("we", "us") sets out below how information about users is handled in the smartphone game "Tiny Biome" (Japanese title: ミニ惑星の生態系; the "App").

1. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information such as your name, address, phone number or email address through the App. We also do not send or store any information about you on our own servers.

2. Information stored on your device

Your progress (the floors you have reached and your clear counts), your purchases made with in-game seeds, and your sound and music settings are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us. They are deleted when you uninstall the App.

3. Advertising

The App uses "Google AdMob", an advertising service provided by Google LLC, and "Unity Ads", an advertising service provided by Unity Technologies (Unity Software Inc. and its affiliates), to show ads (rewarded video ads). Unity Ads is used as a backup when AdMob cannot show an ad. Ads are shown only when the player chooses to watch one, for example to continue from where they left off, to add more animal cards during play, or to redraw a different planet. To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob and Unity Ads may collect and use information such as the advertising ID, IP address, device information and how the App is used. Information collected this way is handled in accordance with the privacy policies of Google and Unity respectively.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Unity Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

In regions where it is required, the App shows a consent screen (Google's consent management platform) at first launch or similar. You can review your choice at any time from the App's Settings.

You can opt out of personalized ads, or reset or delete your advertising ID, in your device settings (on Android: Settings → Google → Ads).

4. Disclosure to third parties

We do not provide the information we obtain to third parties, except where required by law. However, for the advertising described above, AdMob (Google LLC) and Unity Ads (Unity Technologies) handle information within the scope described in section 3.

5. Children

The App is not primarily directed at children under 13.

6. Changes to this policy

We may review and change this policy as necessary. Changes take effect from the time they are posted on this page.
